Bring your Poolstation pool controller into Homey.

Poolstation is the cloud platform behind smart chlorinators and pH controllers from Fluidra brands such as Idegis and AstralPool. This app logs in to your Poolstation account (two-factor login codes are supported) and adds every pool as a device.

What you get, depending on what your controller reports:
- Water temperature, pH, ORP (redox), free chlorine, salt and electrolysis
- Target pH, ORP, chlorine and production, adjustable from Homey and from flows
- Relays such as the filter pump and pool light, with the names from your Poolstation account
- Digital inputs, water flow alarm and UV lamp status

Flows: trigger when a relay or input switches, when a water flow problem starts or ends, or when pH, ORP or chlorine changes. Switch relays and set targets from your flows, for example to run the filter pump on surplus solar power.

This is an unofficial app, based on the Home Assistant Poolstation integration by Miguel Camba.
